The creator

About this portfolio

Hello, I'm Muralidhar.

I enjoy combining technology, visual problem-solving and creative tools to make useful digital experiences. This portfolio is being developed as a home for original visual experiments and finished assets.

My interests include clear icon systems, approachable illustrations, dimensional design, small motion details and carefully directed AI imagery.

Creation & quality process

  1. Start with an original brief and visual direction.
  2. Develop drafts and explore variations.
  3. Refine shapes, composition, color and consistency.
  4. Export appropriate formats and test quality.
  5. Document tools, prompts where applicable, and source stages.
Portfolio status: This is the first public portfolio version. The galleries contain starter original vector works and sample motion source files; they are not yet a complete, marketplace-reviewed commercial pack. Finished work, editable source files and genuine creation evidence will be added as they are produced.
